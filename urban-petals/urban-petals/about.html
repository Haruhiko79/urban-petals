for Green Spaces

🌱 Founded 2019

How We Began

From a Balcony Garden to Your Doorstep

In 20, our founder Aaron Mendoza turned his cramped apartment balcony into a thriving urban garden. Neighbors took notice, friends started asking for tips, and soon a small community formed around the shared joy of growing things in the city.

What began as a passion project quickly grew into Urban Petals — a curated online shop dedicated to helping city dwellers bring more green into their homes, offices, and communities.

Today we serve thousands of customers across the Philippines, shipping carefully selected plants, tools, and accessories with the same love and attention that started it all.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Quality First

Every plant and product we stock is hand-selected for health, longevity, and beauty. We never compromise on what we put in your hands.

Customer Joy

Your satisfaction is our garden's sunshine. We go the extra mile to make sure every order arrives in perfect condition and every question gets answered.

Community Roots

We believe urban gardens build stronger neighborhoods. We partner with local growers and give back to urban greening programs each year.

Sustainable Sourcing

We work only with nurseries and suppliers who share our commitment to ethical, eco-friendly growing practices and reduced packaging waste.

Expert Guidance

Every product comes with care tips, and our team of plant enthusiasts is always ready to help — whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned gardener.

Thoughtful Gifting

Plants make meaningful gifts. We offer beautiful gift packaging and personalised notes so your green gift arrives as special as you intended.
